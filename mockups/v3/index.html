The ten showpieces

All ten appear on S1, S2 and D3; subsets elsewhere. Canvas 2D, SVG filters and CSS, no library.

01
Kinetic typePer-character springs toward a target you scrub. The bar parks at the halfway mark so it is easy to grab.
02
Flow fieldTwo thousand particles reading a value-noise field; the pointer cuts a vortex. Trails are compositing.
03
Reaction–diffusionGray–Scott solved on a coarse grid every frame, with a watchdog that restarts it if the reaction burns out.
04
Gooey morphBlur plus a colour matrix that slams the alpha ramp back to a hard edge, so blobs merge like liquid.
05
Spotlight stageObjects shaded by angle and distance to a light you carry, casting shadows away from it.
06
One lineSix drawings from the same 120 points, so the stroke redistributes instead of lifting.
07
Dot matrixInverse-square repulsion on a lattice; clicks send a wave that propagates by distance.
08
ShatterConvex shards from repeated random cuts, kicked by proximity and released by distance so the break travels outward.
09
RibbonA spring-coupled chain extruded perpendicular to its direction of travel, tapering along its length.
10
MurmurationNo field at all. Agents drawn as velocity dashes, with a hairline between every pair currently influencing each other — the negotiation, not the birds.

All five stage formations come from one shared description — the block treatment draws the rectangles, the swarm fills them with particles, the single-line treatment walks their perimeters. Three very different drawings, one source of truth. No GSAP, no Three.js, no scroll library, no build step; the only network request beyond the page is a webfont on D2.
