04 / Working notes

I'd rather ship something correct at 360px than something impressive at 1440.
  1. Design and engineering are one job. The interesting decisions happen where they meet, and they get made badly when there's a handoff in between.
  2. Prototype in the browser. A day of real code settles arguments that three weeks of comments won't.
  3. Accessibility is not a pass. It's the same pass. Retrofitting focus order costs more than building it.
  4. Motion earns its place or leaves. If deleting it loses nothing, it was never doing anything.
