A short argument

Most front-end problems are really decision problems wearing a CSS costume.

The handoff is the bug

Design hands over a picture; engineering builds a thing. Everything interesting — what happens at 360px, what the empty state says, where focus goes after the modal closes — lives in the gap and gets decided by whoever notices last. I work on both sides so there is no gap to fall into.

Correct beats impressive

A layout that survives a long German compound noun, a slow connection and a screen reader is worth more than one that photographs well. Most of my best work is invisible: nothing jumped, nothing trapped focus, nothing took four seconds.

Motion is an argument

Animation should be making a point — this came from there, that is loading, these two are the same object. If the page reads identically with the motion removed, the motion was decoration, and decoration is the first thing to cut.
