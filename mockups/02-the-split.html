About

I'm a front-end engineer in Colorado. Since 2014 I've worked at the seam where design hands off to engineering — and mostly, I've tried to remove the handoff.

What I'm actually good at: making complex interfaces feel inevitable. Data visualization that stays readable at 360px. Motion that carries meaning and gets out of the way. Component systems other engineers can extend without asking me first. And the parts nobody demos — keyboard paths, focus management, reduced-motion alternates, layout stability.

Currently open to senior front-end and design-engineering roles.
