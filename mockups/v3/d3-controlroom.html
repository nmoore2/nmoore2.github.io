Bay 02

Operating notes

Most front-end work is pleasant until the edges arrive: the long string, the empty state, the third concurrent request, the person navigating with a keyboard. Those edges are where interfaces earn their reputation, and they are most of what I do.

I came up through design, so I can own the look when there isn't one yet — and I've been in implementation long enough to know which beautiful ideas cost a team a year.

Everything on this page degrades. Each animation pauses off screen and in a background tab, and has a static state that still communicates when motion is switched off in the panel to your left.

That isn't a caveat on the work. On a page arguing that I'm good at motion, it is the work — anyone can make something move.
