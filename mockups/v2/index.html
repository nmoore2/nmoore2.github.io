Craft wallTen complete components — including a FLIP reorder and a magnetic target that goes inert under reduced motion.
Motion toggle. Every variant has a pause/play control in its header. It starts from your saved choice, falls back to your system prefers-reduced-motion setting, and is seeded before first paint so nothing animates on its way to being told not to — and because the choice outranks the system preference in both directions, someone with Reduce Motion on can still turn the animation on to see it. Paused holds each piece at its fullest state rather than a blank one. Nothing here needs a framework, a bundler, or a network request beyond one webfont in variant B.
