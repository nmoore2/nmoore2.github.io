The contact sheet

Ten techniques at ten different scales. The big ones are the ones worth standing in front of; the small ones are there to show range.

The point of all this

Motion is a language, and most sites only know two words

Fade and slide. They're fine. But an interface that can show where something came from, how two things relate, or that a system is thinking, explains itself without a sentence of copy — and that's a product skill, not a decoration skill.

Everything here degrades

Every piece on this page pauses off screen, pauses in a background tab, and has a static state that still reads when motion is switched off in the header.

Everything here is small

No GSAP, no Three.js, no scroll library. The whole animation layer is a few kilobytes of hand-written canvas and CSS, which is the actual flex.
